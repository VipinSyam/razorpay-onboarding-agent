How the agent works

Rules make every decision that can harm a merchant. AI only reads messy notes and writes messages. Nothing is sent without a human.

Rule: diagnose→ AI: read unclear note→ Rule: accept, route, priority, channel→ AI: write message→ Check: safety checks→ AI: rewrite once if failed→ Human: approve

What AI is never allowed to decide

These are fixed rules (lib/rules.js), the same rules as the Step 1 Excel rules engine.

  • Closing an application (restricted business, R10)
  • Routing to Compliance (R12)
  • Who is recoverable
  • Priority
  • Channel (WhatsApp only if the merchant opted in)
  • Who gets a human call
  • Sending anything: a person approves every message

What AI does

Two jobs only, both checked by rules.

  • Reads the 4 reviewer notes that have no code. The rules accept the reading only if it is high-confidence, and never accept a closure or Compliance reading from the AI alone.
  • Writes the messages in 6 languages (English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi), with an English back-translation for reviewers.

Compliance notes are never sent to any AI.

The safety checks

Every draft is checked before a human sees it. Up to 11 apply to each message, depending on its type (for example, the closure check runs only on closures).
